Who to call for matchmaking

Details come from each body's own contact page, read on 27 October 2026. Only Boxing Victoria names a matchmaker. Everywhere else the contact is the secretary or a general inbox, so ask for whoever arranges bouts. Leagues usually arrange bouts through the promoter or the club running the card, so for a league show call the organiser listed on the event.
